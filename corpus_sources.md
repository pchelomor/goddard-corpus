# Neville Goddard Master Corpus — Sources & Bibliography

## Primary Architecture

This Master Corpus is built from verified primary archives and authoritative digitized transcriptions of the works of Neville Goddard (1905–1972).

---

## 1. Canonical Archives Tiering

### Tier A — Primary Scan / Early Scan & Original Audio
- **Internet Archive (archive.org):** Original first-edition scans published between 1939 and 1966 by DeVorss & Co., Snellgrove Publications, and G. & J. Publishing Co.
- **FeelingField.com:** Digital audio preservation containing 119 original recordings restored directly from reel-to-reel and cassette tapes, matched with verbatim word-for-word transcripts.

### Tier B — High-Quality Digital Corpora
- **TheNevilleGoddard.com:** Primary machine-readable corpus encompassing the 14 core books and 235 lecture records.
- **RealNeville.com:** Extensive text and audio archive (238 text transcripts and historical dates spanning 1948–1972).

### Tier C — Secondary Cross-Check Repositories
- **Neville Goddard\'s Vault (nevillegoddardbooks.com / freeneville.com)**
- **My Neville Goddard (mynevillegoddard.com)**

### Tier D — Bibliographic Authority Indexes
- **Library of Congress / US Copyright Office Historical Records**
- **WorldCat / Google Books / Open Library**

---

## 2. Master Work Classes & Scope

1. **CORE_BOOKS (B001 – B014):**
   - 14 Canonical standalone volumes authored and published directly by Neville Goddard during his lifetime.
2. **LESSON / MASTER_CLASS (S001):**
   - *Five Lessons: A Master Class* (1948), including Lessons 1–5 and the comprehensive Q&A session.
3. **RADIO_TALK (S002):**
   - *The KECA Radio Talks* (1951), 9 broadcast transcripts delivered in Los Angeles.
4. **EDITED_COLLECTION (S003):**
   - *The Creative Use of Imagination* (1952), lecture transcript compilation edited posthumously by Margaret Ruth Broome.
5. **LECTURES (L0001 – L0235):**
   - 235 discovery records cross-verified against RealNeville and FeelingField date registries.
