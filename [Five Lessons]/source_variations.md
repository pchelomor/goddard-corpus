# Source Variations: Five Lessons + Q&A (1948)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **The 1948 Master Class Structure:**
   - Delivered in Los Angeles over five consecutive nights in October 1948:
     - Lesson 1: *Consciousness Is the Only Reality*
     - Lesson 2: *Assumptions Harden Into Fact*
     - Lesson 3: *Thinking Fourth-Dimensionally*
     - Lesson 4: *No One to Change but Self*
     - Lesson 5: *Remain Faithful to Your Idea*
     - Section 6: *Questions and Answers* (Practical audience questions).

2. **Core Methodological Presentations:**
   - Contains Neville's most thorough real-time pedagogical explanations of:
     - The technique of entering the *State Akin to Sleep* (SATS).
     - The psychological interpretation of Jacob and Esau (feeling vs. reality of the senses).
     - The story of Abdullah and Neville's trip to Barbados in first-class ("You are already in Barbados, Neville!").
