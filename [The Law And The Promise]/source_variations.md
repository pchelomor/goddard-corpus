# Source Variations: The Law And The Promise (1961)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Structure: The Law vs. The Promise:**
   - Chapters 1?14 comprise "The Law", presenting dozens of first-person student case histories illustrating the law of assumption in real estate, business, finance, health, and social relations.
   - Chapter 15 comprises "The Promise", where Neville for the first time in a published book reveals the four mystical experiences fulfilling scripture (Resurrection, David as Son, Splitting of the Temple/Serpentine Ascent, Holy Spirit as a Dove).

2. **Case Histories & Empirical Testimonies:**
   - Tagged extensively with `FINANCIAL_CLAIM`, `MEDICAL_CLAIM`, and `REALITY_CIRCUMSTANCES`.
