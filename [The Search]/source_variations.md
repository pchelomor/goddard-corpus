# Source Variations: The Search (1946)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Reprint History in *Resurrection* (1966):**
   - In 1966, Neville republished *The Search* alongside *Prayer, The Art of Believing*, *Feeling Is The Secret*, and *Freedom For All* in the hardcover anthology *Resurrection*.
   - Its original 1946 publication date and standalone monograph status are maintained here under `B006`.

2. **Core Mystical Declarations:**
   - Contains Neville's definitive axiom: *"There is nothing to change but our concept of self."*
   - Contains his famous metaphysical depiction of reality as a cosmic dream: *"The universe which we study with such care is a dream, and we the dreamers of the dream, eternal dreamers dreaming non-eternal dreams."*
