# Source Variations: Resurrection, A Confession Of Faith (1966)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **The Culminating Confession of Faith:**
   - Neville's final major published book essay, setting forth his complete theological synthesis:
     - Chapter 1: *The Gospel of God*
     - Chapter 2: *The Mystery of Golgotha*
     - Chapter 3: *The Unveiling of the Promise*

2. **Theological Synthesis:**
   - Formulates the theological transition from the Law (psychological manifestation) to the Promise (ontological awakening as God).
