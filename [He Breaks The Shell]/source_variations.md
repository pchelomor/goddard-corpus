# Source Variations: He Breaks The Shell (1964)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **The Four Acts of God (The Promise):**
   - *He Breaks The Shell* is structured around the four supernatural acts that unveil the Image of God:
     - Act 1: The Resurrection (Awakening in the skull / Golgotha).
     - Act 2: The Discovery of the Son (David calling man Father).
     - Act 3: The Splitting of the Temple & Serpentine Ascent.
     - Act 4: The Descent of the Holy Spirit as a Dove.

2. **Core Mystical Declarations:**
   - *"Jesus Christ is the true identity of every man."*
   - *"Resurrection is an individual experience, an awakening in one's own skull, followed instantly by a supernatural birth from his skull, a privileged birth in a new creation."*
