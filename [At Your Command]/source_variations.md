# Source Variations: At Your Command (1939)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Reprint Classification vs. Spoken Lecture:**
   - In several online repositories (including early indexes of *TheNevilleGoddard.com*), *At Your Command* was erroneously mirrored in the lecture section.
   - Textual fingerprinting confirms it is word-for-word identical to the 1939 Snellgrove book. In this Master Corpus, the lecture duplicate record is resolved to canonical parent `B001`.

2. **Typography and Capitalization:**
   - The 1939 first edition frequently utilizes capitalized phrases for emphasis (e.g. `DECREE`, `I AM`, `SHALL`, `AT YOUR COMMAND`).
   - These authorial emphases are preserved intact in `quote_original` to maintain fidelity to Neville's original rhetoric.

3. **Biblical Citations:**
   - Neville quotes extensively from King James Version (KJV) Scripture, reinterpreting traditional theology into psychological law.
   - Pure Scripture quotations are tagged with `SCRIPTURE_INCLUDED` and `EXTERNAL_QUOTE` to distinguish Neville's original commentary from biblically attributed lines.
