# Source Variations: Your Faith Is Your Fortune (1941)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Chapter Title Typographical Normalization:**
   - Web archive typography rendered several chapter titles with accidental interior spaces (e.g., 'Yous Hall Decree' for 'You Shall Decree', 'The Principle Oft Ruth' for 'The Principle of Truth', 'Whoa Mi' for 'Who Am I', 'Theb Rea Thof Life' for 'The Breath of Life').
   - In the Master Corpus, all 27 chapter headers are mapped to their canonical 1941 print titles while preserving verbatim paragraph text.

2. **Esoteric Scripture Interpretation:**
   - *Your Faith Is Your Fortune* contains Neville's most comprehensive chapter-by-chapter psychological translation of the Bible (including the Nativity, Crucifixion, Resurrection, Circumcision, Daniel in the Lion's Den, the 12 Disciples, the 23rd Psalm, and Gethsemane).
   - Biblical epigraphs and opening verses are flagged with `SCRIPTURE_INCLUDED` and `EXTERNAL_QUOTE` to differentiate scriptural citations from Neville's original metaphysical commentary.
