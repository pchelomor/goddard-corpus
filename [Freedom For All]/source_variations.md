# Source Variations: Freedom For All (1942)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Chapter Structure & Headings:**
   - *Freedom For All* was published as a practical 9-chapter exposition of the Bible's core psychological law.
   - Chapter headings normalized to canonical titles: *The Oneness of God*, *The Name of God*, *The Law of Creation*, *The Secret of Feeling*, *The Sabbath*, *Healing*, *Desire, The Word of God*, *Faith*, and *The Annunciation*.

2. **Thematic Continuity with 1944 *Feeling Is The Secret*:**
   - Chapter 4 (*The Secret of Feeling*) contains the foundational formulations that Neville expanded two years later into his classic 1944 book *Feeling Is The Secret*.
   - All quotes retain their distinct 1942 phrasing and are cross-indexed to prevent conflation.
