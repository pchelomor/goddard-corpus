## Main editorial actions
- Collapsed repeated I AM / consciousness windows into the strongest standalone formulations.
- Preserved the core metaphysical voice of the book when the wording is clean and self-contained.
- Excluded pure scripture from Neville attribution and held scripture-heavy allegorical interpretation for contextual or spiritual use.
- Kept the most useful formulations of feeling, assumption, desire, the Sabbath, and imaginal listening.
- Excluded categorical healing claims, remote-healing promises, financial guarantees, and passages that could read as universal blame for illness, poverty, accidents, or other people’s behavior.
- Held long Noah, Jacob/Esau, crucifixion, leprosy, mustard-seed, and Immaculate Conception retellings when the explanatory context was more important than a standalone daily quote.
- Retained one concise imaginal-location formulation (“there-ness” / “here-ness”) because it is highly author-specific and practically distinct from the broader assumption material.

## Strongest thematic clusters
1. **I AM / consciousness** — awareness as the central reality and self-definition as conditioning.
2. **Feeling** — the joining mechanism between desire and assumed state.
3. **State change** — moving from a present self-conception to the desired state.
4. **Sabbath / rest** — mental rest following a fixed assumption.
5. **Desire** — desire as a state seeking embodiment and as a reflection of self-concept.
6. **Imaginal listening** — hearing a friend confirm fulfillment as a technique for changing consciousness.
