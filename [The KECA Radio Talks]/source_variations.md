# Source Variations: The KECA Radio Talks (July 1953)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **The 9 KECA Broadcasts:**
   - Broadcast 1: *Be What You Wish, Be What You Believe*
   - Broadcast 2: *By Imagination We Become*
   - Broadcast 3: *Answered Prayer*
   - Broadcast 4: *Meditation*
   - Broadcast 5: *The Law of Assumption*
   - Broadcast 6: *Truth*
   - Broadcast 7: *Stone, Water or Wine?*
   - Broadcast 8: *Feeling Is The Secret (Radio Broadcast)*
   - Broadcast 9: *Affirm the Reality of Our Own Greatness*

2. **Broadcast-Specific Condensation:**
   - Because each talk was designed for a 15-minute commercial radio format, the language is exceptionally direct, punchy, and structured for immediate practical application by a general radio audience.
