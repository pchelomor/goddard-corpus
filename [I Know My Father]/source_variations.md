# Source Variations: I Know My Father (1960)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Chapter Structure & Central Themes:**
   - 10 structured chapters:
     1. *I AM*
     2. *The Name of God*
     3. *The Foundation Stone*
     4. *The Sound of the Trumpet*
     5. *He Who Has*
     6. *The Secret of Prayer*
     7. *The Master Key*
     8. *No Other God*
     9. *Thy Will Be Done*
     10. *Be Ears That Hear*

2. **Radical Monotheism of Consciousness:**
   - Develops the biblical declaration *"Beside Me there is no God"* as the psychological truth that human consciousness is the sole creative cause.
