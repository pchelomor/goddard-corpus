# Source Variations: The Creative Use Of Imagination (1952)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Compilation Provenance:**
   - Compiled by Neville's student Margaret Ruth Broome from notes taken by an attendee at Neville's 1952 San Francisco lecture series.
   - Distinct from first-party books published during Neville's lifetime; classified under `SUPPLEMENTARY` (`EDITED_COLLECTION`).

2. **Thematic Chapters:**
   - 17 structured chapters including *The Pearl of Great Price*, *Self-Remembering*, *The Feeling of "I"*, *Are You Making Wine?*, and *Awake, O Sleeper*.
