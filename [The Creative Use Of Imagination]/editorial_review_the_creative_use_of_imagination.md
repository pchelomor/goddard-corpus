## Attribution constraint

This source requires a stricter attribution rule than a normal Neville book or transcript. Margaret Ruth Broome's preface states that the collection derives from shorthand notes of 1952 lectures, that she elaborated those notes, and that the wording may not reproduce Neville's exact spoken words. The preface itself is Broome's voice and was excluded from Neville attribution.

Accordingly, the SELECT layer is intentionally narrow. It contains only clean, self-contained formulations from the lecture body that express stable core Neville themes. Each selected record is explicitly marked `EDITED_COLLECTION` / `EDITORIALLY_MEDIATED`; the editorial layer does not silently represent this source as a verbatim transcript.
