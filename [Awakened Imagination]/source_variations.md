# Source Variations: Awakened Imagination (1954)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Book vs. 1954 Lecture Series Intro:**
   - Similar to *The Power of Awareness*, online mirrors frequently substituted Neville's introductory spoken lecture to his 19-lecture series in place of the 1954 book.
   - The Master Corpus establishes the complete 8-chapter canonical book *Awakened Imagination*.

2. **The Technique of Revision (Chapter 4):**
   - Chapter 4 (*The Pruning Shears of Revision*) is the historical foundation of Neville's signature method of Revision.
   - All revision passages are tagged with `REVISION` and detailed practical tags.
