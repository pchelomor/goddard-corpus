# Source Variations: The Power Of Awareness (1952)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Book vs. 1953 Lecture Distinction:**
   - On some digital mirrors (including *thenevillegoddard.com/works/the-power-of-awareness/*), the text hosted was actually Neville's 1953 lecture delivered at Dr. Frederick Bailes' Church of Religious Science in Los Angeles.
   - The canonical 1952 book *The Power Of Awareness* contains 27 structured chapters (from *I AM* and *Consciousness* through *The Effortless Way*, *Creation*, *Case Histories*, and *Reverence and Divine Stillness*). The Master Corpus restores the true 27-chapter text.

2. **Case Histories (Chapter 23):**
   - Chapter 23 includes detailed real-world case histories demonstrating the law of assumption in business, health, personal relations, and legal affairs.
   - Tagged appropriately with `FINANCIAL_CLAIM`, `MEDICAL_CLAIM`, and `REALITY_CIRCUMSTANCES`.
