## Decision vocabulary

- **SELECT** — exact Neville wording admitted to the main curated / automatic daily pool.
- **HOLD** — valuable but contextual, spiritually specialized, mixed-source, sensitive, or unsuitable for automatic delivery.
- **DROP** — duplicate, external/scripture, weak/misbounded, unsafe or misleading when isolated, or otherwise unsuitable.

No quota is imposed.
