# Source Variations: Prayer, The Art Of Believing (1945)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Chapter Structure & Headings:**
   - The book is organized into 7 distinct thematic chapters:
     1. *Law of Reversibility*
     2. *Dual Nature of Consciousness*
     3. *Imagination and Faith*
     4. *Controlled Reverie*
     5. *Law of Thought Transmission*
     6. *Good Tidings*
     7. *The Greatest Prayer*

2. **Scientific & Mechanical Analogies:**
   - Chapter 1 introduces the *Law of Reversibility* using analogies from physics, thermodynamics, and electricity (e.g. heat transforming into mechanical motion and vice versa).
   - These passages are tagged with `SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM` and `STRONG_METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM` to denote Neville's conceptual bridges between natural science and the psychology of prayer.

3. **External Poetic Citations:**
   - The epigraph cites Lord Alfred Tennyson's *Morte d'Arthur* ("More things are wrought by prayer than this world dreams of"). Flagged with `EXTERNAL_QUOTE`.
