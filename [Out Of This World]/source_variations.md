# Source Variations: Out Of This World (1949)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Chapter Structure & Core Principles:**
   - The book contains 4 foundational chapters:
     1. *Thinking Fourth-Dimensionally*
     2. *Assumptions Become Facts*
     3. *Power of Imagination*
     4. *No One to Change but Self*

2. **Fourth Dimension & Scientific Metaphysics:**
   - Chapter 1 explores the fourth-dimensional nature of reality, citing dimensional focus, time as space, and the mechanism by which the future is experienced and reshaped before physical manifestation.
   - Tagged with `SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM` and `STRONG_METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM`.
