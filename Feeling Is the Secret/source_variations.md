# Source Variations: Feeling Is The Secret (1944)

## Summary of Textual Variations

1. **Typographical Styling in 1944 Print:**
   - First words of major thematic divisions were typeset in ALL CAPS (e.g. THE, SLEEP, PRAYER, NOT by might).
   - Digital archives normalize these to standard Title Case or preserve uppercase. Both forms are indexed in normalized duplicates.

2. **Neve WordPress Theme Replacement Glitch:**
   - Multiple modern mirrors using the WordPress theme 'Neve' contain an automated regex bug replacing 
eve with copyright metadata.
   - Master text was verified against original 1944 scans, ensuring 100% clean authorial text.

3. **Omission in Abridged Compilations:**
   - Incomplete third-party repositories omitted the garden allegory in Chapter 1 and the critical line 'Signs follow, they do not precede'.
   - Master Corpus includes the complete, unabridged 5,355-word text.
