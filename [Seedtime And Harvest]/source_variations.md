# Source Variations: Seedtime And Harvest (1956)

## Textual Variations & Notes

1. **Book vs. June 10, 1956 Spoken Lecture:**
   - On June 10, 1956, Neville delivered a Sunday morning lecture sharing the book's title (*"Although it bears the same title as the book..."*).
   - The book is a 9-chapter metaphysical treatise (*The Four Mighty Ones*, *The Scale of Being*, *The Game of Life*, *The Water and the Blood*, etc.). Both entries are maintained distinctly in their respective classes (`BOOK` vs `LECTURE`).

2. **The Four Mighty Ones:**
   - Chapter 2 introduces Neville's psychological allegory of the four evangelists/faculties of imagination (Producer, Author, Director, Actor). Tagged with `IMAGINATION` and `STRONG_METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM`.
